How the number is worked out

Rainfall comes from Open-Meteo's hourly forecast for each city. The score follows PAGASA's warning thresholds — 7.5–15 mm in an hour is yellow, 15–30 orange, above 30 red — then adds weight for the 24-hour total, since sustained rain is what floods a catchment. Each city carries a flood-exposure factor drawn from its history: Marikina, Malabon and Navotas flood well before Makati or Muntinlupa on the same rainfall. Once a city appears in a live suspension roundup, the estimate is replaced by it.

Percentages are an estimate from the rainfall outlook, not an official forecast. Only your city government, DepEd, or Malacañang can suspend classes. Announcements are parsed from live news roundups and can lag an LGU post by minutes — confirm on your city's official page before changing your plans.
