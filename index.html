How the number is worked out

Rainfall comes from Open-Meteo's hourly forecast for each city. The score follows PAGASA's warning thresholds — 7.5–15 mm in an hour is yellow, 15–30 orange, above 30 red — then adds weight for the 24-hour total, since sustained rain is what floods a catchment. Each city carries a flood-exposure factor drawn from its history: Marikina, Malabon and Navotas flood well before Makati or Muntinlupa on the same rainfall. Once a city posts a real announcement, the estimate is replaced by it.

Percentages are an estimate from the rainfall outlook, not an official forecast. Only your city government, DepEd, or Malacañang can suspend classes. Announcements below are pulled from live news feeds and can lag a fresh post by a few minutes — confirm on your LGU's official page before changing your plans.
